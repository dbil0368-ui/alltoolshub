PLANNED OFFERINGS

Eight ways SYNORA may serve creators, businesses and developers. These are planned business offerings; purchasing, contracts, API billing and reseller onboarding are not active in this preview.

01

One-time License

A one-time licence for an agreed SYNORA product version or deployment, with clearly defined usage and update terms.

Planned
Explore this model

How it could work

A customer would receive a defined version of a SYNORA product under a written licence. The licence would state permitted users, deployment scope, duration of support and whether updates are included.

Who it may serve

Organisations that prefer a one-time software licence rather than a recurring subscription.

Preparation required

Finalise the product, licence scope, update policy, support terms, security review and an adult-reviewed agreement. No licence is currently offered for sale.

Try a licence scope preview

This creates an informational sample only, not a legal licence.

02

White-label

Offer a customised, partner-branded version of SYNORA for organisations that need their own identity and workflow.

Planned
Explore this model

How it could work

An approved organisation could use a separately configured version with its own name, logo, colours and selected workflows, while the underlying technology and responsibilities are documented.

Who it may serve

Schools, studios, agencies or businesses that need a branded AI workspace.

Preparation required

Build tenant separation, branding controls, privacy and security safeguards, service-level expectations and a reviewed partner agreement. No white-label deployment is available yet.

03

Reseller Program

Allow approved partners to introduce SYNORA to customers under written terms, transparent pricing and support rules.

Planned
Explore this model

How it could work

Approved partners could refer potential customers or resell an authorised SYNORA package. Each partner would have clear rules for referrals, customer support, pricing disclosures and brand use.

Who it may serve

Established IT providers, consultants and software service businesses.

Preparation required

Create partner eligibility checks, training, tracking, support procedures and adult-reviewed reseller terms. There are no active resellers or commissions at present.

04

API Access

Provide documented developer access to eligible SYNORA capabilities with usage limits, authentication and clear billing controls.

Planned
Explore this model

How it could work

Developers could connect their applications to selected SYNORA services through documented endpoints and protected credentials, with rate limits, usage reporting and clear error handling.

Who it may serve

Developers and organisations building their own applications or automations.

Preparation required

Complete stable APIs, authentication, key rotation, abuse prevention, usage limits, monitoring, documentation and a secure billing design. API access and API billing are not active.
