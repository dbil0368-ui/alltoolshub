Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, how information may be handled when you use AllToolsHub.

Information you enter

Many browser-based tools process the text, numbers, or files you provide directly in your browser. Do not submit confidential, sensitive, or personal information unless you are comfortable doing so.

AI and external services

The AI chat feature in this starter version may be unavailable until an AI provider is configured. If it is enabled in the future, prompts may be sent to the configured AI provider for processing and will then be subject to that provider's privacy terms. Some tools may load libraries from third-party content delivery networks. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests their files.

Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted by a third-party hosting provider. The provider may process basic technical logs, such as request details, for security, reliability, and service operation under its own policies.

Children

AllToolsHub is a general-purpose website and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not submit personal details through the site.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as features change. For questions about this page, use the public project repository linked from the website owner where available.
